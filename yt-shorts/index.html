yt-shorts-pipeline

Uses YouTube API Services

A personal tool by Anoshor Paul that uploads short videos he creates to his own two YouTube channels (an AI explainer channel and a stories channel). It is not a public service and has no other users.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service YouTube Terms of Service Google Privacy Policy

How it works

  1. A script writes an original short video script each day.
  2. A GitHub Actions job renders the video (voice, images, captions).
  3. The rendered video is sent to the owner on Telegram for review; he can cancel it.
  4. At the scheduled time, the video is uploaded to the owner's own channel with the YouTube Data API (videos.insert, scope youtube.upload), marked as altered/synthetic content and not made for kids.

Data

Only the owner's OAuth token is stored (encrypted). No viewer or third-party data is collected. Access can be revoked at Google security settings.
